# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, constructive space for users and contributors. Participate
in good faith, welcome differing experience levels, focus feedback on the work,
and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, or
sharing private information.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful content, reject a
contribution, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns through [GitHub's private abuse-reporting
channel](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not use public issues for
reports that identify or endanger another person.

Maintainers will handle reports with discretion, acknowledge receipt when a
response channel is available, and avoid promising confidentiality that the
reporting platform cannot provide.
